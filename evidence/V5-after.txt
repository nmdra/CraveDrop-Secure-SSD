# V5: Driver administrative authorisation after fix

Test source: `security-tests/runtime-v4-v7.mjs`

## Blocked attack

```text
Driver A token PUT /driver/0000000000000000000000b1/availability
HTTP/1.1 403 Forbidden
{"message":"A driver may update only their own availability"}
```

A verified driver cannot change another driver's availability. Anonymous requests are rejected by the existing driver authentication middleware.

## Authorised control

```text
Driver A token PUT /driver/0000000000000000000000a1/availability
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
```

Driver A can still update their own availability. Token prefixes and credentials are not included in the evidence.
